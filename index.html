HOW TO RUN THE ROOM

1 · STOCK THE ROOM

Buy fish and decorations from the SHOP. Everything lands in the holding tray. Tap a tray item, then tap a tank to place it — or drag a decor piece straight onto a tank.

2 · FEED THE RIGHT MOUTH

Predators (200 cm rack) refuse pellets. They eat live prey, bloodworms and fresh shrimp. Nano fish (50 cm rack) eat pellets, bloodworms and shrimp. Food has limited uses; restock before it runs out.

3 · COLLECT THE MONEY

Comfortable adult fish tip paper money into their tank. Click the glowing banknotes to bank them. Fullness, clean water, oxygen and a beautiful aquascape all increase tips.

4 · CLEAN & INSPECT

Open a tank and choose CLEAN to scrub algae from the glass. INSPECT raises a 2.15× water lens with live chemistry read-outs.

5 · BREED A PAIR

Select one adult male and one adult female of the same species. Both must be well fed, the water clean and the pair rested. Eggs hatch into fry, then grow to adults.

CONTROLS

Tap a tank to manage · Esc closes panels · Sound can be muted top-right. The room saves automatically. A 56-colour pixel palette is rendered crisply to 4K displays.
