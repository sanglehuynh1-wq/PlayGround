## 🐠 Thủy Cung — Aquarium Room

A playable 2D pixel-art aquarium management sim. Run a fish room with two 200 cm predator tanks and five 50 cm nano tanks, feed the right food, scrub the glass, inspect water chemistry, breed pairs, and collect the paper money your happy fish leave behind.

- **Shop** — 13 species (Alligator Gar, Redtail Catfish, King Kamfa Flowerhorn, three Channa Maru variants, three Arowana variants, two Betta variants, Panda Cory, wild paradise fish), plus foods and decor.
- **Care loop** — predators need live prey, bloodworms or shrimp (never pellets); nano fish also take pellets. Food is limited-use.
- **Aquascaping** — buy driftwood, rocks and plants, keep them in the holding tray, then drag them into a tank and confirm placement.
- **Economy** — VNĐ wallet, fish sales, breeding fees, and clickable banknotes dropped by comfortable adults.
- **Persistence** — progress saves automatically to `localStorage` under `thuycung_aquarium_v1`.

### Run

```bash
./start.sh
```

Open the printed local URL. No build step or dependencies are required — the game is plain HTML, CSS, Canvas 2D and ES modules.

### Controls

Tap/click a tank to open its management view. `Esc` closes panels, `H` opens the handbook, and sound can be muted from the top bar. Built for desktop, tablet and phone.
